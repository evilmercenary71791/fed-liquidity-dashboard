Data methodology

Official data are downloaded automatically after deployment. Manual CSV import is also supported: one row per observation, with a date and values in USD billions. Optional primary_credit and btfp columns support emergency-lending analysis. The dashboard aligns the data by the dates you supply. All automatically downloaded series use Wednesday H.4.1 levels for the weekly reserve bridge.

Reserve bridge: Δreserves = ΔFed assets − ΔTGA − Δreverse repos − Δother nonreserve liabilities and capital. The residual captures omitted liabilities and measurement differences.

Fed asset categories are components of total assets. TGA, reverse repos, and reserves are liabilities. The dashboard does not add those liabilities to total assets.

Official sources: H.4.1 · WALCL · FRED
